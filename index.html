ABOUT COSMOS

COSMOS is an advanced 3D solar system & spacecraft exploration platform built using Three.js, Vanilla ES Modules, custom GLSL shaders, and WebGL physics.

Experience photorealistic planetary atmospheres, solar plasma dynamics, 17 natural moons, and 50+ artificial satellites and deep space missions including Sputnik 1, Aryabhata, Hubble, JWST, Mangalyaan, Aditya-L1, NISAR, and Starlink.

CONTROLS & SHORTCUTS

🖱️ Mouse Controls

  • Left Drag: Orbit camera around target
  • Right Drag: Pan camera position
  • Scroll Wheel: Zoom in / out
  • Click Planet / Moon / Spacecraft: Select celestial body & focus
  • Click Empty Space: Deselect object

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • W / A / S / D: Translate camera position
  • Space / Shift: Elevate / lower camera altitude
  • R: Reset camera to Solar System overview
  • ESC: Close active panel or modal
